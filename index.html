Attention tone

A sound that keeps you on task.

Switch it on for a list and a soft tone plays while you run it. A little pressure, so you don’t drift off to another app. It stops the moment the last item is ticked.

Say it. Get a checklist.

Safe

Your lists never leave your phone.

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything you write lives in your device’s own memory, and it works offline. Only the AI quick list sends your words out, and only when you use it.

Put it on your device.

ChecklistPro is a web app. Install it once and it opens like any other app, full screen and even offline. No app store needed.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Windows

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Click Install. It lands in the Start menu.

Mac

  1. In Safari: File, then Add to Dock.
  2. In Chrome: the install icon in the address bar.
  3. It opens from the Dock or Launchpad.

In the app you can also tap Settings, then Install app, for the same steps.
