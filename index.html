    From cockpits to your pocket.

    Born in the cockpit

    In 1935 a new bomber crashed on take-off, because it was too complex to remember. Pilots answered with a short list, and aviation never stopped using one.

    Proven in the operating room

    Hospitals borrowed the idea, and surgical and intensive-care checklists have been studied for the lives they help protect.

    Made for ordinary days

    Packing, leaving home, the gym bag, launch day. The same trick, built for one thumb.
